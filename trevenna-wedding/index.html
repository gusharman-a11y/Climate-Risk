The Venue

Exclusively yours, from first arrival to farewell brunch

Trevenna sits in 142 acres of countryside at St Neot, within an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty on the edge of Bodmin Moor. When you book, every barn, garden and view belongs to you and yours alone.

  • Three licensed indoor ceremony spaces, plus outdoor ceremonies under the Arbour
  • The Mowhay with orchard views · the stone-walled Threshing Barn · the Garden Venue
  • Accommodation for 45 guests across restored barns and the Farmhouse
  • Up to 120 day guests and 150 evening guests
  • In-house kitchen cooking with homegrown and Cornish produce

Good to Know

Questions couples ask us

How many guests can we bring?

Up to 120 guests for the day and 150 in the evening, with accommodation on-site for 45 across the restored barns and Farmhouse.

Can we marry outdoors?

Yes — under the wood and slate Arbour with countryside views, or in the gardens. We also hold three licensed indoor ceremony spaces, so the weather never gets a vote it can win.

Do we have to use your caterers?

Food and drink is one of the things Trevenna is loved for — our in-house kitchen cooks with homegrown ingredients and the best of Cornish produce, and menus are shaped with you, from formal feasts to relaxed hog roasts.

What does “exclusive use” actually mean?

Exactly what it says. For your one, two or three nights there are no other bookings, no other couples and no strangers at breakfast — every barn, garden and view is yours.

Where exactly are you?

At St Neot, near Liskeard in south-east Cornwall — inside an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty on the southern edge of Bodmin Moor, and easier to reach than it feels once you're here.
